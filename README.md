This repository contains code focusing on the extraction of non-parametric galactic morphological measurements. 
The purpose of this research is to explore the effects of the surrounding environment on galaxy mergers and galaxy
formation at high redshift. The results of this research are published in the research notes of the American Astronomical Society.
The link to the published paper is linked [here](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/2515-5172/addec5) .
